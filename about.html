Official Launch Announcement

Introducing XANTA: Redefining Luxury Streetwear & Heavyweight Essentials

Today marks the official debut of XANTA, an independent streetwear label engineered to establish an uncompromising standard in modern minimalist luxury streetwear and heavyweight boxy tees.

In an era saturated with fast-turnaround trends and compromised synthetics, XANTA was founded on a singular premise: build timeless, structural essentials engineered to endure daily wear without losing form.

What We Create

  • Architectural Heavyweight Tees: Cut from premium 280 GSM combed cotton jersey, featuring dropped shoulders, ribbed 1.25-inch crew collars, and pre-shrunk architectural drape.
  • Minimalist Monochrome Essentials: Restrained, functional staples stripped of loud branding, focusing entirely on weight, drape, and stitch density.
  • Limited-Run Capsule Pieces: Small-batch releases engineered for collectors who value substance, longevity, and structural discipline over seasonal noise.

Dedicated Delivery to the USA, UK & Canada

From day one, our fulfillment infrastructure is built to deliver directly to clients across the United States, the United Kingdom, and Canada. Every order is fulfilled with tracked, priority dispatch to ensure a seamless experience from checkout to unboxing.
