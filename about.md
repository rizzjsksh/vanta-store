## Our Origin & Ethos
XANTA was founded on a singular premise: modern streetwear has become oversaturated with transient hype, disposable synthetic blends, and oversized logos. 

We build the antithesis: **heavyweight, architectural garments stripped of distraction.**

Every silhouette is drafted from raw geometrical proportion. By focusing exclusively on a monochrome palette — stark whites, deep reactive blacks, and structural charcoal — we redirect all attention to the cut, the weight of the weave, and the drape of the garment on the body.

## The 280 GSM Textile Standard
We do not use generic blanks. Our t-shirts are milled from bespoke 280 GSM (8.25 oz/yd²) combed ring-spun organic cotton jersey:

1. **Weight & Structure**: 280 GSM creates a natural drape that hangs cleanly away from the body without clinging or bunching.
2. **Durability & Form**: The high yarn density prevents neck stretching, hem curling, and torquing across laundry cycles.
3. **Pre-Shrunk Treatment**: Every bolt of fabric is treated before cutting to guarantee dimensional stability upon receipt.
4. **Collar Engineering**: Our 1-inch rib crewneck is bound with double-needle topstitching to resist sagging.

## Sustainable Longevity
We reject fast-fashion waste cycles. By crafting resilient heavyweight staples that survive seasons of continuous wear, we encourage a smaller, higher-quality capsule wardrobe. 

Our packaging is 100% recyclable, made from post-consumer recycled paperboard and compostable protective bags.
