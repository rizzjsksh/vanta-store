## Frequently Answered Questions

### Where does XANTA ship?
We ship to the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada with end-to-end tracking.

### What is your return and exchange policy?
We offer a 30-day return and exchange policy from the date of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags intact. Sizing exchanges are free of charge.

### How should I care for my 280 GSM tee?
Machine wash cold (30°C / 86°F) inside-out with like colors. Line dry or tumble dry low. Do not bleach. Warm iron on reverse if desired.
