## Brand Identity & Story
XANTA started with a simple frustration: the basics we reached for every day were either too precious to live in or too plain to care about. So we set out to make the other kind.

Heavyweight cotton, honest washes, cuts that hold their shape after the fiftieth wear. Nothing loud. Nothing disposable. Just clothes that do their job, worn the way you actually live.
